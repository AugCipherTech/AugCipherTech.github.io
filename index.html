FAQ

Questions? Answers.

How long does it take to build a website or software?

A business website typically takes 1–3 weeks. Ready products like POS, pharmacy or restaurant systems can be customised and deployed in 2–4 weeks. Fully custom software and ERP depend on scope — we give a clear timeline after discovery.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so we provide a fixed, transparent quote after understanding your needs. The first consultation is completely free.

Will the software work offline?

Yes — our POS and restaurant systems can work offline and sync automatically when the internet is back.

Do you support bKash, Nagad and local couriers?

Absolutely. We integrate bKash, Nagad, SSLCommerz, bank payments, SMS gateways and couriers like Pathao, Steadfast and RedX.

Do I own the source code?

For custom projects, yes — full ownership is transferred to you. For SaaS products you get a licence with continuous updates.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes free support. After that, flexible monthly maintenance plans keep your system secure, updated and growing.
